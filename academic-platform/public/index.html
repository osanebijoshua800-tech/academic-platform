Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started.

How do I earn and download my completion certificate? ▼

Once you enroll in a course as a student, you can access the certificate option inside your classroom view and download a high-resolution PDF certificate directly from your browser.

What is the difference between Student and Tutor accounts? ▼

Students can view course modules, submit assignment files, participate in live chat rooms, and generate certificates. Tutors have additional permissions to create assignments, publish instructions, and review student submissions.

Are live classrooms and chats updated in real time? ▼

Yes! Built with Socket.IO technology, messages and activity updates are instantly broadcasted to all active participants in the classroom room without needing to reload the page.
